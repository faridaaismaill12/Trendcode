Labs

Every lab runs entirely in your browser. No sign-up, no installs. Each one has a few missions. Finish them all to complete the lab, earn 25 XP and move along your learning path.

How labs work

  • Missions guide you through each experiment. They tick off automatically when you do the thing.
  • Progress is saved in this browser only, so there's no account and nothing leaves your device. Clearing site data resets it.
  • Each lab pairs with an explainer. Read first, or dive in and read afterwards. Both work.
