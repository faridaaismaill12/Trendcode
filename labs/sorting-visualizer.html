Sorting Race

Two algorithms, the same shuffled data, one step per tick. Every comparison and swap counts. Watch Big-O stop being a formula and become something you can see.

Beginner~10 minNot completed yet

Cheat sheet

AlgorithmTypicalWorstGood at
Bubble sortO(n²)O(n²)Teaching. Little else.
Selection sortO(n²)O(n²)Few swaps
Insertion sortO(n²)O(n²)Small or nearly-sorted data: O(n) best case
Merge sortO(n log n)O(n log n)Predictable, stable
Quick sortO(n log n)O(n²)Fast in practice, with a smart pivot

This lab's quick sort uses the last element as the pivot, the textbook version. Real libraries pick pivots more cleverly to dodge the worst case.
