Train a Neuron

One artificial neuron, two inputs (the x and y position of a point), and gradient descent. Click to place points, press train, and watch the neuron learn where to draw the line.

What's happening inside

  • The neuron computes p = sigmoid(w₁·x + w₂·y + b): a weighted sum squashed into a probability between 0 and 1 that the point is class B.
  • Each training step measures the loss (how wrong the probabilities are), works out which way each weight should move to reduce it, and nudges them all a little. That's gradient descent.
  • A single neuron can only draw a straight line. XOR and the ring can't be split by one line. Real networks fix this by stacking neurons in hidden layers, which learn useful features like x·y on their own.
